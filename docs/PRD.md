\# SpendWise Product Requirement Document



\## Product Name



SpendWise



\## Vision



Help people understand their spending and discover opportunities to save money.



\## Problem



People know their expenses but don't know where they can save without reducing their lifestyle.



\## Solution



SpendWise analyzes bank statements, categorizes transactions, and provides personalized money-saving recommendations.



\## Target User



People who use UPI and digital payments.



\## MVP Features



1\. Upload bank statement

2\. Extract transactions

3\. Categorize expenses

4\. Analyze spending patterns

5\. Generate savings recommendations

6\. Monthly financial report



\## Future Features



1\. Real-time payment suggestions

2\. UPI integration

3\. Smart alternatives

4\. Budget planning
